Our Story

About The Shoe Hub

The Shoe Hub is the women's footwear house— a Kampala-based retailer dedicated to bringing curated, well-crafted heels to the modern Ugandan woman.

Why we exist

Finding heels that combine comfort, durable craftsmanship, and quietly elegant design in one place is harder than it should be. We travel, source, and personally select every pair we stock — so each silhouette earns its place on the shelf.

What we stand for

  • Considered design over fast fashion.
  • Honest pricing — no inflated luxury tax.
  • Personal styling advice from real humans, in real time.
  • Supporting and uplifting African retail talent.

Logistics

Delivery & Returns

Delivery

  • Within Kampala: Free same-day or next-day delivery on orders above UGX 150,000.
  • Across Uganda: 1–3 business days via trusted courier partners. Fees calculated at checkout.
  • East Africa: 4–7 business days. Contact us for a shipping quote.

Returns & Exchanges

  • 7-day hassle-free returns on all unworn items in original packaging.
  • Exchanges for size or colour are free within Kampala.
  • Sale items are final unless faulty.
